# The MIT Cookbook

This cookbook emphasizes health, convenience, and a variety of options to cater to different dietary restrictions and preferences.
Most of the meals provided here are based on the hit TV series "ONE PIECE".

**Visit the MIT Lunchroom to learn more, or click on the above links to create your own food!**

## Project Goals:

* Provide a collection of healthy and delicious recipes inspired by "One Piece".
* Offer a variety of meal options to suit different tastes and dietary needs.
* Create a user-friendly interface for easy navigation and recipe discovery.
* Promote healthy eating habits within the MIT community.
